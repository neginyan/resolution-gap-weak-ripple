\emph{Rudin--Shapiro bands.}
$f=1+c\cos t+c\sum_{k<W}\epsilon_k\cos((m+k)t)$ with the Rudin--Shapiro signs $\epsilon_k$, $W=2^j$.
Non-negativity follows from $|P_j|^2+|Q_j|^2=2W$.
The blurred sup is bounded by Abel summation with the partial sums $(2+\sqrt2)\sqrt n$.

\emph{Counterexamples to conjecture C.}
Add a coherent block (all phases zero) of $N_c$ harmonics at $u_c=0.3$ with raw amplitude $A$.
Non-negativity follows from $1-c(1+\sqrt{2W})-A(1+N_c)\ge0$; (P2) and (P3) hold by construction.

\emph{Search for counterexamples to Lemma S.}
The search draws gapped and gap-free windows with $1$ to $300$ harmonics on each side, coherent or random, and hill-climbs on the worst margin.
We used $300$ windows of each kind; the smallest margins were $6.7\times10^{-3}$ and $1.3\times10^{-2}$.
