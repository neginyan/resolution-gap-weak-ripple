\section{Split sandwiches}
\label{sec:sandwich}

The $\eta$ sandwich of~\cite{F} (Table~2 and App.~A there) reads
\begin{equation}
\frac{\langle Y^2\rangle}{1+\eta}\le\Bigl\langle\frac{Y^2}{h}\Bigr\rangle\le\frac{\langle Y^2\rangle}{1-\eta}
\end{equation}
for $Y=h_w$ and $Y=h_t$.
It costs a factor $(1-\eta)/(1+\eta)$ in the bound.
A coherent low-frequency block can make $\eta$ large while carrying almost no energy, and then this factor is far too pessimistic.

Split $R=R_{\rm lo}+R_{\rm hi}$, where $R_{\rm lo}$ has frequencies in $[1,M]$ and $R_{\rm hi}$ frequencies $\ge m$.
Let $Y=\phi(w)y(t)$ with $y=R$ or $y=R'$, and $h=1+\psi(w)R$ with $0\le\psi\le1$.
Let $\langle\cdot\rangle$ be the period average integrated against a positive measure in $w$.
Put
\begin{equation}
f_{\rm lo}=\frac{\|Y_{\rm lo}\|^2}{\|Y\|^2},\quad
\eta_{\rm lo}=\sup|R_{\rm lo}|,\quad
\eta_{\rm hi}=\sup|R_{\rm hi}|,
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\kappa=\min\Bigl(\eta_{\rm lo},\ \frac{\sup|y_{\rm hi}|\,\|R_{\rm lo}\|_t}{\|y_{\rm hi}\|_t}\Bigr),
\end{equation}
\begin{multline}
E=\eta_{\rm lo}f_{\rm lo}+2\eta_{\rm hi}\sqrt{f_{\rm lo}f_{\rm hi}}\\
+(\eta_{\rm hi}+\kappa)f_{\rm hi},
\end{multline}
\begin{equation}
B=\bigl(\eta_{\rm lo}\sqrt{f_{\rm lo}}+\kappa\sqrt{f_{\rm hi}}+\eta_{\rm hi}\bigr)^2 .
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Lemma S] \proofst
\label{lem:S}
If $2M<m$ (a spectral gap), then
\begin{multline}
\|Y\|^2\max\Bigl(\frac{1}{1+\eta},\,1-E\Bigr)\le\Bigl\langle\frac{Y^2}{h}\Bigr\rangle\\
\le\|Y\|^2\min\Bigl(\frac{1}{1-\eta},\,1+E+\frac{B}{1-\eta}\Bigr).
\end{multline}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use $1/(1+r)=1-r+r^2/(1+r)$ with $0\le r^2/(1+r)\le r^2/(1-\eta)$, and expand $Y^2r$.
Two of the six products vanish because of the gap:
$\langle Y_{\rm lo}^2r_{\rm hi}\rangle$ and $\langle Y_{\rm lo}Y_{\rm hi}r_{\rm lo}\rangle$.
The others are bounded by Cauchy--Schwarz.
For the product $\langle Y_{\rm hi}^2r_{\rm lo}\rangle$ use $\langle y_{\rm hi}^2|R_{\rm lo}|\rangle_t\le\sup|y_{\rm hi}|\,\|y_{\rm hi}\|_t\|R_{\rm lo}\|_t$.
This is where the energy $\|R_{\rm lo}\|_t^2=S_{0,\rm lo}/2$ of the low block replaces its sup.
For $r^2$ use Minkowski; the term $\|Y_{\rm hi}r_{\rm lo}\|$ is bounded with $\|y_{\rm hi}R_{\rm lo}\|_t\le\sup|y_{\rm hi}|\,\|R_{\rm lo}\|_t$ (or by $\eta_{\rm lo}$), so the same $\kappa$ enters $B$ as $E$.
The line-by-line proof, with the norms and measures, is in App.~\ref{app:details:S}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Lemma S$'$, no gap] \proofst
\label{lem:Sp}
Without the gap, Lemma~\ref{lem:S} holds with $E$ replaced by
\begin{equation}
E'=E+\eta_{\rm hi}f_{\rm lo}+2\eta_{\rm lo}\sqrt{f_{\rm lo}f_{\rm hi}} .
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two products that used the gap are bounded by $\eta_{\rm hi}\|Y_{\rm lo}\|^2$ and $\eta_{\rm lo}\|Y_{\rm lo}\|\|Y_{\rm hi}\|$.
The upper bound $B$ used Minkowski only, so it is unchanged.
\end{proof}

\emph{Scaling.}
Let $\lambda=\min(\mathrm{low}_{S_0},\mathrm{low}_{S_2})$ and $\Upsilon=\mathrm{up}_{S_2}$ be the lower and upper factors of the two channels.
The part of $\mathrm{den}$ that carries $S_0$ is given its factor $1/(1-\bar\eta)=2$ directly in the cells of Sec.~\ref{sec:cert}.
Write the lower bound of Lemma~R* as $P_{\rm bg}+\mathrm{low}_{S_0}P_0+\mathrm{low}_{S_2}P_2-N$ and its den as at most $d_{\rm bg}+\Upsilon d_2$.
Here $P_{\rm bg}$ collects the gains without a sandwich factor, $P_0,P_2\ge0$ the gains of the two channels, $N\ge0$ the remainders, and $d_{\rm bg},d_2\ge0$.
All of them depend on the state only.
Since $P_0,P_2,d_{\rm bg}\ge0$ and $\Upsilon\ge1$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathrm{Num}-\frac{\lambda}{\Upsilon}\gamma\sigma\,\mathrm{den}&\ \ge\ T(\lambda),\\
T(\lambda)&:=P_{\rm bg}+\lambda(P_0+P_2)-N\\
&\qquad-\lambda\gamma\sigma(d_{\rm bg}+d_2).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
$T$ is affine in $\lambda$, so $T\ge0$ on $[\lambda_*,1]$ follows from $T(1)\ge0$ and $T(\lambda_*)\ge0$.
Every window of Theorem~\ref{thm:H1} has $\eta\le\sum q0_n\le\Qb=1/4$ by (P3), so its lower factors are at least $1/(1+\eta)\ge4/5$ (the $\eta$ sandwich, and the first entry of the maximum in Lemma~\ref{lem:S}), and its upper factor is at least one.
We take $\lambda_*=1/(1+\bar\eta)=2/3$.
$T(1)\ge0$ is the certificate with the factors set to one.
$T(\lambda_*)\ge0$ is a second certificate, with the factors $(\lambda_*,\lambda_*,1)$ and the target $\lambda_*\gamma$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:cert}).
Together they give $g\ge(1-\bar\varepsilon)(\lambda/\Upsilon)\gamma$ for every admissible window.
The step cannot be shortened to ``a certificate with the factors set to one gives $g\ge(\lambda/\Upsilon)\gamma$'': that would need $-N\ge-\lambda N$, which fails for $\lambda<1$.
In general $T(\lambda)=\lambda T(1)+(1-\lambda)(P_{\rm bg}-N)$, and the second certificate is what controls $P_{\rm bg}-N$.
Versions~1.0 and~1.1 stated the shortened step; see the version paragraph in Sec.~\ref{sec:theorem}.

\begin{corollary}[Corollary S] \proofst
\label{cor:S}
If the certified target is $T_{10}=1+12.1/\ell+2.5\times10^{-4}$ and $(\lambda/\Upsilon)T_{10}\ge1$, then $g\sqrt\ell\ge1$ (times $1-\bar\varepsilon$).
\end{corollary}

\emph{Checks.}
On the nine windows that refute conjecture C, $(\lambda/\Upsilon)T_{10}$ lies between $1.0020$ and $1.1074$ \numst.
These are exact sums over the window.
A search for counterexamples to Lemmas~\ref{lem:S} and~\ref{lem:Sp} is described in App.~\ref{app:windows}.
It covered low and high frequencies, few and many harmonics, coherent and random phases, and used a hill climb.
It found no violation.
The same search does find violations of deliberately wrong versions in which $\eta_{\rm lo}f_{\rm lo}$, $\eta_{\rm hi}f_{\rm hi}$ or $B$ is dropped.
It finds none when the term $2\eta_{\rm hi}\sqrt{f_{\rm lo}f_{\rm hi}}$, the term $\kappa f_{\rm hi}$ or the gap-free cross term is dropped.
Whether those three terms are needed is therefore not settled.

\emph{Picture.} A low-frequency block is like a slow swell under the fine ripple.
It can be tall, but the lens reads the ripple's detail through its energy, and a slow swell carries almost none of the detail.
