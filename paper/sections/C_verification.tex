\section{Verification}
\label{app:verification}

\code{verification/run\_all.py} recomputes a sample of every certificate in less than thirty minutes; \code{--recompute} recomputes all of them.
It also re-derives every number of the manuscript from \code{results/} and checks that it appears in the text.
Lemma~BE is checked by \code{ch\_bedge.py}; the local corrections of Sec.~\ref{sec:open:tau} by \code{ci\_rtheta.py}; the single columns with Lemma~A* by \code{ci\_link.py}.
The covering of Sec.~\ref{sec:cert} is checked by \code{cq\_cover.py} ($10^5$ random states and eleven damaged tables, in the default run); sample columns of the $\mu$ tails and of the wedge are recomputed by \code{cq\_core.py}, whose term functions are regenerated by \code{cq\_make.py} and compared with the stored ones; the new steps are checked by \code{cp\_checks.py} and \code{cq\_checks.py}.
The results on the narrow focus (Sec.~\ref{sec:open:tau}, App.~\ref{app:joint}) are produced by \code{cm\_counter.py}, \code{cm\_local\_bound.py}, \code{cm\_checks.py}, \code{cn\_lemmas.py}, \code{cn\_search.py}, \code{co\_remainders.py}, \code{co\_rtheta.py} and \code{co\_envmin.py}.
The acceptance rules of App.~\ref{app:cells} are implemented in \code{cl\_cells.py} and \code{cl\_link.py}; the bounds are checked against long double by \code{ck\_rounding.py}, and the $\tau$ cells and Lemma~BE by \code{cl\_tau\_be.py}.
Each step of App.~\ref{app:details} is checked independently, in exact fractions and $60$-digit decimal, by \code{ck\_details.py}.
The stage scripts are listed with their outputs in the repository README.
